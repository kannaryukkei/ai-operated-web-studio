Visitors may be reacting to weak cooling, water leakage, overdue cleaning or planned split-AC installation. They want to know which service category fits, what can be diagnosed remotely, what affects final cost and what to prepare before inspection.

The scenario suggests that the main concern is uncertainty: a customer may not know whether a routine clean or technical inspection is appropriate. The website should explain this distinction before presenting a next step.
