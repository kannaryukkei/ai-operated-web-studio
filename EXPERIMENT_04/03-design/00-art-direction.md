# Art Direction — ACFIX Studio

**Version:** 1.0 — experimental fixture  
**Input:** WRP-EXP04-0001 (DRAFT; any design/build is limited to an explicitly disclosed simulation)  
**Status:** PREVIEW CONCEPT / NOT CLIENT APPROVED

## Design fingerprint

**Aircare field manual** — an architectural/technical editorial language built around airflow, temperature, equipment sections and clear service boundaries. The page should feel like a precise field guide, not a generic home-service landing page.

### Composition
- A very large off-center typographic masthead with a single line-drawn wall-mounted AC unit and visible airflow paths.
- A horizontal service decision strip that reads as three different service routes, not three identical cards.
- Pricing shown as a compact comparison ledger with caveats adjacent to each starting value.
- A technical explainer diagram showing indoor unit / filter / airflow as abstract elements, not an actual customer system.
- Process as a linear annotated path; numbered markers only for true sequential steps.
- Coverage as plain text, no fabricated map pin or address.
- FAQ in open, readable rows; final contact area is a non-interactive unavailable state.

### Visual tokens
- Background: pale mineral #F3F2EC
- Primary ink: #17252B
- Secondary slate: #4A5B60
- Technical cobalt: #2457D6
- Soft blue tint: #E2E9F5
- Divider: #C8D0D0
- Display and body: deliberately selected system sans typography; no external font fetch.

### Responsive behavior
- 1440px: asymmetric wide diagram composition; service routes in an editorial three-column band.
- 768px: two-column service composition, stacked price rows.
- 390/320px: one column; service labels remain adjacent to descriptions/prices; no hidden critical caveats; accessible collapsed menu.

### Asset plan
All diagrams are inline SVG/CSS, authored for this experiment. They illustrate concepts only, not actual units, service results, staff or premises. No photography, stock, external embeds or people.

## Differentiation check

- ZACKS: dark teal automotive photo hero, vehicle-class pricing selector and auto-detailing story. ACFIX uses light technical editorial composition, airflow diagrams, distinct service routes and a clear estimate ledger.
- Semburat Bakehouse: warm handmade product-editorial photography and menu/order pattern. ACFIX avoids product retail, bakery narrative and cart interactions.
- RUANG RAPI: home-organizing service with human intake and missing contact. ACFIX uses technical HVAC decision information and a field-manual visual language rather than home-organizing categories.
 
This is a material composition/IA/visual-language distinction, not a palette swap.
