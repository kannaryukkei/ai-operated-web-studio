# Policies & Customer Journey — ACFIX Studio

**Source ID:** SRC-FI-004  
**Version:** 1.0 · **Evidence:** DEMO_ONLY · **Publication:** PENDING / INTERNAL ONLY

## Customer journey

1. Customer selects a service need or describes a symptom.
2. Customer shares unit type/capacity, location and access details.
3. ACFIX checks the coverage zone and requests any missing information.
4. A visit window is proposed; it is not confirmed until a separate human confirmation.
5. Technician inspects the accessible unit/site.
6. Technician explains the scope and any additional cost.
7. Customer explicitly approves the work and estimate.
8. Work is performed within the approved scope.
9. Technician demonstrates the operating check and provides a simple job summary.
10. Customer pays and receives a receipt.

The website must distinguish a request/consultation from a confirmed appointment.

## Payment

- Routine cleaning, deep cleaning and initial inspection: cash, bank transfer or QRIS after work is completed.
- Installation: 30% deposit after the written estimate is approved; balance after installation and function check.
- No payment information is collected on the website.
- No online payment gateway or payment confirmation is implemented.

## Cancellation and rescheduling

- Standard cleaning/inspection: free cancellation or rescheduling up to three hours before the agreed arrival window.
- After the crew has departed for the address, a Rp50.000 visit/travel fee may apply if the customer cancels or cannot provide access; this must be explained before dispatch.
- Installation: deposit is fully refunded if ACFIX cancels. If the customer cancels more than 24 hours before the agreed date, 100% of the deposit is refunded. Within 24 hours, the deposit is retained only to cover materials already purchased or third-party costs actually incurred; any remaining balance is refunded with an itemized explanation.
- Rescheduling depends on team capacity and is not guaranteed until confirmed.

## Workmanship warranty

- Routine cleaning/deep cleaning: seven calendar days for recurrence of a drain-flow issue directly caused by the cleaning work, subject to inspection.
- Installation labour: thirty calendar days for defects directly attributable to installation workmanship.
- Manufacturer warranties for AC units and purchased components follow the respective manufacturer/seller terms and are not issued by ACFIX.
- Exclusions include pre-existing faults, refrigerant leaks, electrical problems, component failures, building drainage problems, misuse, physical damage after service and conditions outside the performed scope.
- Warranty review does not guarantee that the unit will achieve a particular cooling temperature or energy-consumption result.

## Safety and work boundaries

- Technician controls isolation of power before opening the unit when required.
- Customer must provide safe access and disclose known building restrictions.
- No live electrical repair, refrigerant handling, ceiling construction, structural modification or work at unsafe height is implied by routine cleaning.
- Additional/specialist work needs a separate assessment and written approval.

## Privacy and data handling (benchmark assumption)

- Customer contact and address details are used only to coordinate the requested service.
- In this synthetic website benchmark, no form data is collected or transmitted. The primary CTA opens a local demo interaction that displays a sample message and says clearly that nothing is sent or stored.
- No analytics, advertising pixels, CRM, online chat service or customer database is connected.
