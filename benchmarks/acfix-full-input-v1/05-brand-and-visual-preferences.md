# Brand Strategy & Visual Preferences — ACFIX Studio

**Source ID:** SRC-FI-005  
**Version:** 1.0 · **Evidence:** DEMO_ONLY · **Publication:** PENDING / INTERNAL ONLY

## Brand foundation

- Name: ACFIX Studio.
- Descriptor: Perawatan AC Rumah & Kantor Kecil.
- Tagline candidate: **“Nyaman di rumah, jelas dari awal.”**
- Positioning: a clear, considerate AC-service option that explains scope and cost before doing additional work.
- Primary value: clarity and confidence in choosing a service—not an unsupported promise of superior performance.
- Brand personality: calm, capable, warm, straightforward, tidy and contemporary.
- Voice: practical Indonesian, short explanations, helpful without being overfamiliar or overly technical.

## Audience and emotional target

Primary audience:
- Homeowners and household decision-makers aged roughly 28–55 in the named service zones.
- Apartment residents/tenants who need to coordinate access.
- Owners/managers of small offices and retail premises.

The visual experience should make them feel:
- “I can understand which service to ask about.”
- “The scope and price are explained before extra work.”
- “I know what to prepare and what happens next.”

Avoid fear-based language about dirty air, sickness, energy savings or equipment failure unless a supported source explicitly justifies the statement. Do not promise health, savings, cooling or lifespan outcomes.

## Visual preferences from the fictional owner

Preferred:
- Warm, daylight-filled residential interiors.
- Rich, large-scale photography/visuals as a focal point, not tiny decorative thumbnails.
- Deep forest/teal as an anchor, warm off-white as breathing space, and a controlled citrus/lime accent.
- Expressive modern sans-serif typography.
- Strong contrast between large visual moments and concise practical information.
- Sections that each have their own composition, provided the journey remains clear.
- Friendly iconography with a consistent line weight, used selectively.
- Rounded corners only where they support the image/interaction; not a uniform radius on every element.
- Motion only for meaningful transitions or feedback.

Avoid:
- Generic HVAC blue/red palette.
- Cream + serif newspaper/editorial aesthetic as the default.
- Repeated split hero, repeated three-card rows, identical cards everywhere, or small icons before every line.
- All-caps eyebrow labels above every section.
- Arbitrary section numbering, fake data dashboards, gauges, air-quality readings or diagnostic selectors.
- Stock photographs that falsely imply real ACFIX premises/technicians/customers.
- Excessive gradients, soft shadows, glassmorphism, scroll-jacking and decorative animations on every element.

## Layout preferences

- Desktop: image-led opening with a strong visual focal point, flexible/asymmetric composition, clear offer hierarchy and direct paths to service details/pricing.
- Tablet: preserve the main visual/story rather than merely shrinking columns.
- Mobile: decisive reading order, large readable tap targets, service/pricing comparison without excessive sideways scrolling, and a clear contact action.
- Content density: moderate. Do not pad a one-page site with unnecessary sections.
- Avoid making a website feel like an admin dashboard or printed technical manual.

## Reference preference — learning, not copying

Use these references as research starting points. Extract one relevant design lesson from each and state what must not be copied:
- Dyson Air Treatment: https://www.dyson.com/air-treatment — product/technology storytelling and large explanatory visuals; do not copy product claims, device imagery or brand identity.
- Climat: https://climat.com.au/ — category clarity and service information; do not copy its IA wholesale or imply its product/service range.
- Poppy Cake Baking Company: https://sahlitdesign.com/work/poppy-cake-baking-company — cohesive visual identity and confident use of typography/photography; unrelated category, use only as craft reference.
- Morrow & Crumb: https://alexvannelli.com/work/morrow-and-crumb/ — restraint and original illustrative identity; do not reuse bakery motifs or its composition.
- Land-book: https://land-book.com/ — browse for strong composition and visual variety; no single website is the target.

## Design autonomy

The owner has supplied preferences, not a wireframe. The Studio must decide the actual art direction, section compositions, typography, grid, motion and responsive behavior. Two materially different creative directions must be explored before selection. The owner does not want to approve CSS details or choose between near-identical palette variants.

## Explicit no-go claims

Never state “teknisi bersertifikat”, “terbaik”, “nomor satu”, “hemat listrik hingga X%”, “udara lebih sehat”, “pasti dingin”, “garansi semua kerusakan”, “respon 24 jam”, or other unsupported claims. Use only the service, process and policy facts in the source packet.
