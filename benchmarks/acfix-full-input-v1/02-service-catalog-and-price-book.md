## Supported equipment

- Wall-mounted residential/light-office split AC.
- Capacity: 0.5–2.5 PK.
- Supported refrigerant type is checked from the unit nameplate; this price book does not promise refrigerant compatibility or include refrigerant.
- Excluded: central AC, VRV/VRF, chiller, cassette, ducted, portable, window AC, cold storage and industrial systems.

## Inclusions and exclusions

### Cuci Rutin
Included:
- Floor and nearby furniture protection within the immediate work area.
- Cleaning of removable filters and accessible covers.
- Controlled cleaning of accessible evaporator surfaces.
- Drain tray and drain flow check.
- Basic operating check before and after the service.
- Wastewater collection and immediate work-area cleanup.

Not included:
- Refrigerant top-up or leak repair.
- Electrical repairs, PCB replacement or compressor work.
- Replacement filters, drain hose, insulation or other spare parts.
- Indoor-unit full disassembly beyond routine access.
- Scaffolding, difficult ceiling access or wall repair.

### Deep Cleaning
Included:
- All applicable routine-cleaning steps.
- More thorough accessible coil and drain cleaning.
- Removal and cleaning of selected accessible components according to unit design.
- Operating check and a short work summary.

Not included:
- Full invasive disassembly of sealed components.
- Refrigerant work, leak repair, electrical/component replacement or spare parts.
- Repairs unrelated to cleaning.
- Special-height equipment or structural access work.

### Pemeriksaan Awal Gangguan
Included:
- Customer symptom interview.
- Visual inspection of accessible indoor/outdoor unit components.
- Basic operating observation and initial findings.
- Written estimate or recommendation for the next appropriate action.

Not included:
- Definitive diagnosis where specialist testing or disassembly is required.
- Spare parts, repair work, refrigerant or electrical modifications.
- Guaranteed identification of every intermittent fault in one visit.

The inspection fee is not automatically credited against repair. Any credit must be offered explicitly in a separate approved quotation; this benchmark offers no credit.

### Pemasangan Unit Baru — jasa
Included:
- Standard installation labour for one wall-mounted split unit.
- Position check and installation consultation.
- Mounting and connection work using the materials listed in the customer-approved quotation.
- Vacuuming and basic function check when the selected installation package includes the required materials/equipment.

Not included by default:
- New AC unit purchase.
- Copper pipe, insulation, cable, drain hose, bracket, breaker, socket and other materials unless separately itemized in the quotation.
- Core drilling through reinforced concrete, wall finishing, electrical circuit upgrades, pump installation, scaffolding or unusual access.
- Removal/disposal of the old unit.

## Price presentation rules

- Show the starting/size-based amount beside its inclusions/exclusions.
- Never show a single “all-in” installation price because materials and site conditions vary.
- State that additional work requires an estimate and customer approval first.
- Do not imply all jobs take the same duration.
- Do not invent discounts, bundles, “best seller” badges, limited slots or savings claims.
- Do not treat these figures as real market pricing.
