## 2. Business intelligence

### Business model

Fictional local service business offering cleaning, deep cleaning, initial inspection and new-unit installation for wall-mounted split AC units (0.5–2.5 PK) in selected Surabaya and Sidoarjo districts.

### Primary customer jobs

- Understand whether the need is routine maintenance, deeper cleaning, initial fault inspection or new installation.
- Know the likely service price and what the price includes.
- Understand which additional costs may arise before work is approved.
- Confirm service area, travel surcharge, schedule and access requirements.
- Prepare the unit/location so the visit can proceed safely and efficiently.

### Audience groups

1. Homeowners and household decision-makers.
2. Apartment residents and tenants who need building-access coordination.
3. Owners/managers of small offices, studios, salons and shops.

### Purchase triggers

- Routine maintenance interval reached.
- AC is visibly dirty or has an odor.
- Dripping water, weak cooling, abnormal sound or intermittent shutdown.
- A new split AC unit needs installation.
- A property manager needs maintenance planned outside peak business hours.

### Main anxieties / objections

- Hidden costs and scope expansion after arrival.
- Unclear differences between routine cleaning and troubleshooting.
- Uncertain service availability or district coverage.
- Damage, mess or poor access coordination.
- Confusion about refrigerant, spare parts and warranty boundaries.

### Supported differentiator

**Explain the work and estimate before additional work begins.** The service model explicitly separates cleaning, initial inspection and installation. It requires customer approval before additional work. These are synthetic process facts, not independent real-world proof.

### Trust approach

There is no usable social proof. Trust should be established through visible scope, itemized pricing logic, approval-before-extra-work policy, transparent limitations, operational information and clear process. Do not replace unavailable proof with badges, testimonials, review scores, years-in-business claims or staff profiles.
