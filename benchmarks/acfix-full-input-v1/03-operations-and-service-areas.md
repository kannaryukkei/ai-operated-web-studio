# Operations & Service Areas — ACFIX Studio

**Source ID:** SRC-FI-003  
**Version:** 1.0 · **Evidence:** DEMO_ONLY · **Publication:** PENDING / INTERNAL ONLY

## Operating hours

- Monday–Saturday: 08.00–17.00 WIB.
- Sunday and Indonesian national holidays: closed.
- Standard arrival windows: 08.00–10.00, 10.00–12.00, 13.00–15.00.
- Last standard job start: 15.00 WIB.
- Requests should be submitted at least two hours before the desired slot.
- No real-time calendar or availability integration exists in this benchmark; never imply a specific slot has been reserved.

## Team and daily capacity

- One service crew of two technicians.
- Maximum planning capacity: three routine-cleaning/inspection jobs per day, or one installation plus one light service job.
- Jobs can be rescheduled for severe weather, unsafe access, missing building permission, or a preceding job that materially exceeds its estimate.
- The website may explain these constraints but must not invent technician names, portraits, years of experience, qualifications or staff biographies.

## Area coverage

### Zone A — no standard travel surcharge in this fixture

**Surabaya:** Gubeng, Genteng, Tegalsari, Simokerto, Bubutan, Tambaksari, Kenjeran, Bulak, Mulyorejo, Sukolilo, Rungkut, Tenggilis Mejoyo, Wonocolo, Wonokromo, Sawahan, Gayungan, Jambangan, Dukuh Pakis, Sukomanunggal, Krembangan.

**Sidoarjo:** Waru, Gedangan, Sedati, Buduran and Sidoarjo town.

### Zone B — Rp25.000 travel surcharge per visit

**Surabaya:** Sambikerep, Lakarsantri, Pakal, Benowo, Asemrowo, Tandes, Karangpilang, Wiyung and Gunung Anyar.

**Sidoarjo:** Taman and Tanggulangin.

### Outside standard service area

Gresik, Krian, Porong and areas not named above are outside standard coverage for this fixture. Do not claim service availability there. An exception may only be quoted manually and must not be implied by the public coverage list.

### Building/access conditions

- Apartment/office customers must arrange building access and work permission before the visit.
- Parking, lift booking, security registration and loading-area costs are borne by the customer if charged by the property.
- High-ceiling or difficult outdoor-unit access requires separate assessment.
- No service is performed where technician safety cannot be reasonably maintained.

## Customer preparation

Before a routine visit:
- Share AC type and capacity (if known).
- Describe the symptom or maintenance need.
- Send a clear photo of the indoor unit and outdoor-unit location when possible.
- Clear approximately one metre around the indoor unit.
- Protect or move fragile items beneath the work area.
- Arrange access to the outdoor unit and building permission.
- Keep children and pets away from the working area.

Before installation:
- Share room size and intended indoor-unit position.
- Share proposed outdoor-unit position and approximate pipe route.
- Confirm wall/material access, power availability and building restrictions.
- Do not drill walls or modify electrical wiring before the technician's assessment.

## Operational truthfulness

The website can state the above as fixture facts but cannot:
- claim a live schedule, booked slot or guaranteed arrival;
- promise service outside the named districts;
- imply an active team or actual technician identity;
- promise every unit can be serviced without inspection;
- imply travel, parking, materials or access charges are always included.
