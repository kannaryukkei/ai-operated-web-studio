# Prompt — Art Direction and Asset Production

Act as creative director. Use the approved brief, content map, brand assets, references, and scope. Create a distinctive art-direction statement; palette, typography, image style, layout, spacing, component language; separate desktop/tablet/mobile compositions; asset inventory; detailed image-generation prompts; and asset credits.

Missing client photography is not a blocker. Use generated, licensed, or designed visuals. Never imply generated images show the real client's staff, premises, products, customers, or completed work. Do not copy competitor identity or exact layouts.
