# Prompt — Lead Qualification and Discovery

Act as a website strategist and delivery analyst. Normalize the lead and discovery notes. Summarize business, audience, offer, customer problem, and primary conversion. Recommend Starter / Business / Custom / Not a fit with explicit scope signals (page count, persistence, CMS, auth, payment, integrations). Produce the discovery brief, missing questions, claims requiring client approval, and proposed visitor journey.

Do not price the project or promise delivery dates. Human approval is required for tier, scope, price, and timeline.
