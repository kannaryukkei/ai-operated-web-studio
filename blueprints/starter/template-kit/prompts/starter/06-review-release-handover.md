# Prompt — Preview, Release, Handover and Calibration

**Master Skill mapping:** Stage 8 (release and handover) and Stage 9 (evidence-led calibration).

Read the approved scope, WRP, project dashboard, source/fact/asset/CTA records, QA report, exact candidate commit, preview record and approval register. Do not infer approval from silence or a green build.

## Stage 8 — Review and release

**G4 — Preview acceptance**
- Prepare preview URL, exact version/commit, customer-question coverage, known limitations, disclosed concept assets and a consolidated review request.
- Obtain the authorized client/business review of customer-facing accuracy, offers, policies, public destinations, agreed visual direction and the exact preview.
- Record PREVIEW_ACCEPTED for that exact version, or CHANGES_REQUESTED with a consolidated issue list.
- Classify feedback as defect, included revision or change request. Owner approval is required for new cost, scope, timeline or exception.

**G5 — Production readiness**
- Verify the Starter Product Acceptance Gate/project acceptance criteria, source-to-page coverage, content and publication statuses, asset rights/consent, CTA behavior, responsive/accessibility/visual evidence, build/tests, SEO/metadata, domain/hosting ownership, costs, rollback and handover readiness.
- Obtain final client/business confirmations for public facts, content, destinations and permissions.
- Obtain the Studio owner's production-readiness decision. Record untested areas as NOT_TESTED; no blocker is silently waived.
- State `PRODUCTION_READY` only when all required production blockers are resolved.

**G6 — Production release authorization**
- The Studio owner explicitly authorizes the exact release candidate and any spend, DNS, domain or publishing action.
- The client/business owner approves live public content, rights, destination behavior and account/domain arrangements according to the agreement.
- Record `RELEASE_AUTHORIZED` before deployment. Execute only the authorized steps.
- Mark `RELEASED` only after the actual production deployment and production URL/CTA verification evidence are recorded.

**G7 — Handover and closure**
- Prepare the agreed source/repository access, domain/hosting ownership, account and asset register, recurring costs/renewals, limitations, backups/rollback, support boundaries and usage notes.
- Obtain client confirmation of receipt, access and ownership responsibilities; obtain owner closure decision.
- Mark `HANDED_OVER` only when the exact handover evidence and ownership/access confirmation are recorded.

## Stage 9 — Evidence-led calibration

Record root cause (input gap, reasoning/prompt gap, implementation defect, QA escape, scope/commercial issue or external dependency), evidence, minimal correction, regression tests and impact on delivery time/cost/quality. Update the Master Skill, prompt, skill or template only when the lesson is reusable and evidenced. Do not turn one-off preference into a system rule.

## Safety and truthfulness

Do not send client messages, spend money, alter DNS, publish, transfer credentials or account ownership without explicit authorization. Never include secrets in project artifacts. Do not fabricate business facts or social proof, including portfolio/demo testimonials, ratings, awards, metrics, customer counts or case results. Concept imagery is not evidence and must not imply real client work.

## Required output

G4/G5/G6/G7 status and evidence; exact preview/production version; release decision owner; deployment and production verification result; handover record; calibration finding and regression evidence; unresolved support/care items. Do not describe a prepared draft as a completed external action.
