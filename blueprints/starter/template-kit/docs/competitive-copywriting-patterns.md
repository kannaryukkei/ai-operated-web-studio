# Competitive Copywriting Patterns — Starpoint Garage Reference

## Purpose

Record the content and conversion patterns observed in the Starpoint Garage website text supplied for the ZACKS Starter redesign. Use these patterns as strategic references, not as copy to duplicate.

## What Starpoint does well

1. **A specific point of view.** "Every bike deserves care" is a clear philosophy. It makes the site about thoughtful ownership and care, not simply a list of wash prices.
2. **Customer self-identification.** The visitor can recognize their vehicle type and its needs before choosing a service.
3. **Treatment-led explanations.** Each service has a purpose, description, indicative time/price, and next action.
4. **Education before conversion.** The site explains categories, material sensitivity, special surfaces, and differences between treatments.
5. **Trust through process.** Assessment, transparent scope, experienced handling, and quality checks make the work feel considered.
6. **Proof and ownership experience.** Real reviews, original workshop photos, customer updates, and a member app add confidence.
7. **Booking clarity.** The page explains what happens after submitting an inquiry, how price and availability are confirmed, and what is not automatically booked.

## How ZACKS should adapt those patterns

ZACKS has a different proposition: it is a mobile home-service detailing business for both cars and motorcycles. Its strongest story is convenience with clear expectations.

Recommended content sequence:

1. **Hero — convenience + outcome:** explain that detailing comes to the customer's location, and that the customer can keep their day moving.
2. **Vehicle understanding:** let visitors choose car or motorcycle and understand the S / M / L / XL price class.
3. **Core treatment menu:** show Express, Signature, and Deep Detail with changing prices for the selected vehicle class.
4. **Additional treatments:** show verified catalog additions such as Interior Only, Paint Polishing, Coating, and Odor Removal, with careful price caveats.
5. **Philosophy and standards:** communicate need-based recommendation, scope/price agreement before work, and customer inspection before payment.
6. **Visual gallery:** keep the gallery visually prominent. Licensed stock or generated images may be used for concept presentation, but must be identified as references rather than ZACKS customer work.
7. **Process:** explain inquiry, scope/schedule discussion, inspection and price approval, then execution and customer check.
8. **Home-service readiness and service area:** show Surabaya, Sidoarjo, Gresik and explain water, electricity, and safe/adequate work space requirements.
9. **WhatsApp inquiry form:** compose a clear message to the admin; explicitly state that the website does not store submitted information and that an inquiry is not a confirmed booking.
10. **FAQ and close:** resolve remaining questions, then offer a direct conversation.

## Do not copy Starpoint's unsupported-for-ZACKS claims

The supplied Starpoint content describes its own motorcycle-first workshop. Do not transfer these claims to ZACKS:

- Physical walk-in workshop at Kendangsari or workshop opening hours.
- Starpoint's motorcycle-only positioning, service names, price matrix, and estimated treatment durations.
- Member application, account, service history, live job status, and promotion features.
- Starpoint's customer reviews, rating, testimonials, or photo documentation.
- Helmet care, repaint, wheel repaint, valet, transport, or other services not confirmed in the ZACKS catalog.
- Starpoint's deposit, payment, rescheduling, or booking rules.

## Evidence boundaries for ZACKS

- Package prices and add-on starting values are sourced from the audited design-intelligence-v1 frontend fallback catalog. Verify against the current Laravel catalog and owner approval before production release.
- Vehicle classes and example models are guidance, not a universal classification rule; final class is confirmed by the admin.
- ZACKS is represented as home service, with base location in Wonorejo, Surabaya, and service areas in Surabaya, Sidoarjo, and Gresik.
- Customer preparation includes access to water and electricity plus a safe, sufficiently spacious work/parking area.
- The website's consultation form only creates a prefilled WhatsApp message; it stores no booking data and does not confirm a booking.
- Genuine customer testimonials and completed-work claims must never be invented or passed off as verified. For a portfolio-only demo, clearly labelled fictional customer-story scenarios and sample business metrics are acceptable as demonstration content; these must be replaced or explicitly approved before an operational launch.

## Design direction

Keep ZACKS's dark charcoal / warm bone / teal identity, but build a richer editorial experience with high-impact photography, clear vehicle selection, a treatment catalog, pricing transparency, purposeful section variation, and a more complete inquiry journey. Avoid generic repeated card grids and avoid copying Starpoint's exact layout or brand language.
